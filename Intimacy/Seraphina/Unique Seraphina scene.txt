Seraphina Unique House Scene
Unlocked only when Seraphina is fully leveled (15) and the Ember Throne Citadel is bought and equipped.
This is a separate, one-time scene. It builds through the house exterior and interior, then switches to the unique image at the end. The final image is permanently saved as a memory even after reincarnation. Seraphina is present. No other companions speak.
Tone: Cheeky, lustful, and competitive. She treats every room like an invitation and every glance like a wager. The Hero tries to match her, which only makes her lean in harder.
Exterior Approach
Scene: Ember throne citadel exterior.
Action: Dusk. The Hero stands on the cracked stone path leading to the Ember Throne Citadel. Black towers rise against a burning sky, windows glowing orange, embers drifting across the rocks. Seraphina steps up beside him, one hand on her hip, looking at the open doors with a slow smile.
SERAPHINA (smiling): “Hot enough to melt the hinges. I like it. Most contracts I’ve signed only burned until morning. This one looks like it intends to stay lit. You brought me here on purpose.”
HERO: “I thought you’d like the heat. Want to see if the inside matches?”
SERAPHINA (soft): “Lead. I’ll decide how long we stay once I see the throne.”
Interior
Scene: Ember throne citadel interior.
Action: Firelight fills the grand hall. Tall columns frame a raised throne in front of a massive hearth. Cracks in the floor glow with slow lava. Seraphina walks ahead, pausing once to trail her fingers along the back of a chair, then turns near the steps. Her smile is sharper.
SERAPHINA (smiling): “I can feel the heat through the stone. You’re showing me a room built for one person to sit higher than everyone else. I’m already thinking about who ends up on their knees first.”
HERO: “You’re assuming it’s you.”
SERAPHINA (determined): “I’m not assuming. I’m offering. Keep walking if you want. The longer you look, the more I’ll charge for the next room.”
Unique Scene
Scene: Unique seraphina intimate.
Action: Candlelight fills the dark bedroom. Seraphina stands with one hand braced against the wall, body turned, looking back over her shoulder. Black lace clings to her, long hair falling down her back. She watches the Hero step into the doorway, gold eyes bright.
SERAPHINA (smiling): “There you are. I’ve been waiting. Thinking about how you’d look once I got you in here. You keep trying to stay composed. I can already feel how hard you’re fighting it. I want to see who breaks first.”

SERAPHINA (soft): “I do. Come closer if you think you can last. Or stay there and watch me. Either way, I’m not leaving this room until one of us gives in. Your choice. I’m already so hot just thinking about how long you’ll try to hold out.”
She shifts her weight, pressing her hips back slightly, fingers curling against the wall. Her smile never fades.
SERAPHINA (determined): “Well? Are you going to keep teasing, or are you going to come over here and find out who lasts longer? I already know what I want. I’m just waiting to see if you can keep up.”
