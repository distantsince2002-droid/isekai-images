Nia Intimacy Scenes
These trigger separately at bond levels 5, 10, and 15. Each scene is independent. Dialogue builds through ocean and dock scenes and shows the bond strengthening, then switches to the intimacy image at the end. The final image of each scene is permanently saved as a memory even after reincarnation. Nia is present. No other companions speak.
Scene 1 — Date (Level 5)
Scene: Ocean (calm).
Action: Evening. The Hero walks the docks with Nia. She leans against a barrel, one hand on her hip, watching the ships. The light is low over the water. Her tail flicks once.
NIA (smiling): “Harbor’s quieter once the last boats tie off. Most crews only come through when the tide’s still running. I check the ropes myself. They slip if you leave them.”
HERO: “You always walk the docks after?”
NIA (soft): “When the cargo’s in. Keeps the crew from betting on the wrong things. You can walk with me if you want. The next pier has better shade.”
HERO: “I’ll come. Show me which ships you checked.”
NIA (smiling): “Stay close. The planks are wetter than they look.”
Scene: Nia intimacy scene 1.
Action: She looks over at the Hero, still leaning on the barrel. The ships and sunset stand behind her.
Unlock: Memory saved — Nia Date.
Scene 2 — Romantic Outing (Level 10)
Scene: Ocean (calm).
Action: Evening. Nia stands on the dock, one hand raised, calling out to her crew on the ship. The Hero stands nearby, watching. Ships rock in the low light. Her tail flicks as she shouts.
HERO (internal): She doesn’t raise her voice for nothing. The crew moves when she speaks. Even from here, it’s clear she runs the deck. She’s been watching me the same way since the last run.
NIA (smiling): “Lines tight! Don’t leave the bow open! You—move it before the tide turns!”
She turns toward the Hero, still grinning.
NIA (soft): “They listen better when someone is watching. You’re welcome to stay. The deck’s warmer once the sun drops.”
HERO: “You run them hard.”
NIA (smiling): “Come closer. I want to see if you can keep up.”
Scene: Nia intimacy scene 2.
Action: She looks toward the Hero, one hand still raised. The ships and sunset stand behind her.
Unlock: Memory saved — Nia Outing.
Scene 3 — Intimate (Level 15)
Scene: Ocean (night).
Action: Full night. Nia leads the Hero below deck on the ship. Lantern light glows in the small cabin. She stops near the bunk, looks back, and grins. Her tail flicks slow. She steps closer.
NIA (smiling): “You followed me down. Most people stop at the deck. I’ve been watching you since the last run. I take what I want. Right now, that’s you. Don’t look so nervous.”
HERO: “Nia… wait—”
NIA (determined): “No waiting. I’ve already decided. Come here.”
She moves fast, pinning the Hero down against the bunk, hands on either side of him, leaning in close.
NIA (soft): “There. Better. I’ve been thinking about this since the docks. You’re not getting away now. Look at me.”
HERO: “You’re… really close.”
NIA (smiling): “That’s the point. I’ve wanted you under me since you stepped on my ship. Stop fidgeting. I like it when you get quiet.”
HERO: “Nia…”
NIA (determined): “Stay still. I’m not done looking.”
Scene: Nia intimacy scene 3.
Action: She pins the Hero down, looking down at him with a wide grin. Lantern light glows in the cabin.
Unlock: Memory saved — Nia Intimate.
No further unlock. Each scene ends independently.
