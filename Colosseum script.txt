Colosseum. Fifteen levels. Level 15 is the champion. Completing all fifteen before the daily reset triggers the Emperor’s congratulations and the soul-shard reward. Each picture has an Action under it. Speech is in quotation marks. Unlock means the reward is granted. No unlock means nothing joins.
The Hero does not answer every line.
Approach
Scene: Colosseum exterior. Action: The Hero climbs the steps between the braziers. A line of fighters waits under the arch. The Emperor is not among them.
EMPEROR: “New stamp. The sand does not care how many roads you have walked. It only cares whether you leave it standing. First gate is open. Survive fifteen and the house pays in more than coin. Fail early and the stands forget you by morning.”
HERO: “Open it.”
No unlock.
Level 1 — Alpha dire wolf
Scene: Colosseum arena interior. Action: The sand is empty. The far gate rises. A large grey wolf steps out, head low.
EMPEROR: “First test. It does not circle. It takes the throat or it dies.”
Scene: Alpha dire wolf. Action: The wolf charges. He meets it and does not chase it into the wall.
No unlock.
Level 2 — Armoured rhino
Scene: Colosseum arena interior. Action: The next gate opens. An armoured bulk is already moving.

Level 3 — Basilisk
Scene: Colosseum arena interior. Action: Coils fill the centre of the sand before he is fully through the gate.
EMPEROR: “Do not meet the eyes for long. The stands like a statue. I do not.”

Level 4 — Beastkin warrior

HERO: “He kept his feet longer than the animals.”

Level 15 — Colosseum champion

EMPEROR: “Last door. Beat him and the house owes you. Lose and you start the ladder again tomorrow.”

Completion before daily reset
Scene: Colosseum arena interior. Action: The champion is down. The Hero is still standing. The Emperor has come to the rail. The sun has not set.
EMPEROR: “Fifteen, and the day is not finished. The house does not see that often. Take the shards. They open doors the Guild will not stamp.”
HERO: “Shards. Not a title.”
VOICE: “Soul shards. Enough of them summon companions the roads do not offer.”
Unlock: Soul shards granted. No companion joins on the sand.
