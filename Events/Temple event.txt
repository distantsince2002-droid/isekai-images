Temple Event: Evening Prayers
Unlocked after the correct reincarnation. No combat. The player meets Hana at the shrine and completes a sequence of interactions to unlock her. Choices appear only where they matter. Wrong choices do not fail the event; they simply change the immediate reply. Hana is present. No other companions speak.
Arrival
Scene: Temple event exterior.
Action: Dusk light filters through the trees. Paper lanterns glow along the stone path. The Hero walks under the red torii and continues up the steps toward the hall. A few visitors remain on the path ahead. No fight in this picture.
HERO: “Shrine’s open late. Quieter than the roads.”
HANA (neutral): “Most people leave before the last lamps are lit. You’re early for that.”
HERO: “Didn’t come for the lamps. Came because the path was open.”
Inside
Scene: Temple event interior.
Action: The Hero steps into the wooden hall. Lanterns hang from the beams. The polished floor reflects the light. Hana stands near the altar steps, carefully adjusting a vase of blossoms. She turns as the Hero approaches.
HANA (smiling): “You’re not from the usual evening crowd. Most people bow and leave without speaking.”
HERO: “I don’t bow unless I mean it. You keep this place?”
HANA (neutral): “I keep the lamps lit and the offerings fresh. The rest looks after itself. You’re welcome to stay if you don’t mind the quiet.”
HERO: “Quiet’s fine. What needs doing before you close?”
HANA (determined): “The water basin outside needs refilling before the last visitors leave. And the blossoms on the altar are starting to drop. If you help with those, I’ll show you the side path people usually miss.”
Choices:
1. Help with the basin first.
2. Start with the blossoms.
3. Offer to do both.
4. Ask what the side path is first.
HANA (soft): “Either order works. Just don’t knock the offerings over. The fruit is for the morning, not the floor.”
Basin
Scene: Temple basin scene.
Action: They step back outside. The stone basin sits under a small wooden roof, water already low. Hana lifts a bucket from the well beside it and pours carefully while the Hero steadies the edge. Lantern light catches the ripples. Visitors continue up the steps in the background.
HANA (neutral): “You didn’t spill any. Most people rush and half of it ends up on their boots.”
HERO: “I don’t rush water. You do this every night?”
HANA (smiling): “Most nights. It keeps people from walking in with dirt on their hands. Small thing. Still matters.”
HERO: “Small things add up. Blossoms next?”
HANA (determined): “Blossoms next. They’re already falling. If we leave them, the floor will be covered by morning.”
Altar
Scene: Temple altar scene.
Action: They return inside. Hana removes the wilting blossoms from the vase and replaces them with fresh ones. The Hero holds the vase steady. Offerings of fruit remain undisturbed on the low table in front of the carved altar. Soft light from the open windows touches the floor.
HANA (soft): “Hold it steady. These are the last from the trees out front. Once they’re gone, we use the dried ones until spring.”
HERO: “They’re still open. You pick them yourself?”
HANA (smiling): “I do. Early, before the visitors arrive. It’s quieter then. You can come with me next time if you want. Or you can just help when you’re here.”
HERO: “I’ll help when I’m here. Side path still open?”
HANA (neutral): “It is. It leads behind the shrine, past the old stone markers. No one goes there after dark unless they’re with me.”
Side path
Scene: Temple side path scene.
Action: Hana leads the way along a narrow stone path behind the hall. Wooden fences line both sides. Lanterns grow farther apart. She stops near a weathered stone marker and turns back. The distant torii and shrine roof are still visible between the trees under the fading sky.
HANA (determined): “The markers here are older than the shrine. I come out when the evening prayers finish. It’s quieter. You can come back if you want.”
HERO: “You offering the path, or asking me to stay longer?”
HANA (soft): “Both. I’ve been here long enough. The shrine doesn’t need me every night. If you’re going further, I can come. I know the old paths better than the roads.”
HERO: “You sure? You seem settled here.”
HANA (smiling): “Settled isn’t the same as finished. I know the roads, the markers, and where the water runs clean. That’s useful if you’re travelling. And I’d like to see more than the same trees every evening.”
HERO: “Then come. The shrine can manage a night without you.”
HANA (neutral): “I’ll leave word. Don’t expect me to pray over every campfire. And don’t expect me to stay quiet if you take a bad path.”
HERO: “Fair. I don’t take bad paths on purpose.”
HANA (soft): “Good. Then we start tomorrow. Or whenever you leave. I’ll be ready.”
Unlock: Hana is unlocked and joins the party.
No further unlock. The event ends.
