Kira Intimacy Scenes
These trigger separately at bond levels 5, 10, and 15. Each scene is independent. Dialogue builds through town scenes and shows the bond strengthening, then switches to the intimacy image at the end. The final image of each scene is permanently saved as a memory even after reincarnation. Kira is present. No other companions speak.
Scene 1 — Date (Level 5)
Scene: Town street (night).
Action: Evening. The Hero walks the upper street when Kira falls in beside him. She has her hands in her overalls, tail flicking as she matches his pace. The town lights are starting to show below the wall.
KIRA (smiling): “Wall’s better than the market stairs. Fewer people asking questions. Come on, I’ll show you the view before the lamps fill up.”
HERO: “You always take the high path?”
KIRA (neutral): “When I can. The old woman doesn’t check up here. The stones stay warm longer than the alleys. Watch the edge.”
HERO: “You know these streets well.”
KIRA (soft): “Had to. Sit if you want. The drop’s steeper than it looks, but the lights are worth it.”
HERO: “I’ll stay.”
KIRA (smiling): “Good. Don’t fall. I’m not climbing down after you.”
Scene: Kira intimacy scene 1.
Action: She leans on the wall, chin in her hand, looking over at the Hero. The town lights spread out behind her.
Unlock: Memory saved — Kira Date.
Scene 2 — Romantic Outing (Level 10)
Scene: Store and market (base scene).
Action: Night. Kira is already at the tavern counter when the Hero arrives. She slides a mug toward him and leans forward, grinning. The place is half-empty, lanterns low.
KIRA (smiling): “Thought you’d take longer. I already started. First one to finish their mug picks the next round. Loser pays.”
HERO: “You’re already ahead.”
KIRA (soft): “Then catch up. The ale burns, but it keeps the ears from going flat. Come on. I want to see if you can keep up.”
HERO: “Fine. What’s the stake?”
KIRA (smiling): “Winner chooses where we go after. Loser doesn’t get to complain. Drink.”
Scene: Kira intimacy scene 2.
Action: She leans on the counter, mug in hand, grinning at the Hero. The tavern lights glow behind her.
Unlock: Memory saved — Kira Outing.
Scene 3 — Intimate (Level 15)
Scene: Town street (night).
Action: Full night. The Hero reaches the quiet building. Kira is already inside, sitting on the edge of the bed. The door is open, lantern lit. She looks up when he steps in, a little loose from the drinks earlier, tail flicking slow.
KIRA (smiling): “I’ve been waiting. Door’s open so you wouldn’t miss it. Come here.”
HERO: “You… had a few already?”
KIRA (soft): “A couple. Enough to stop waiting. Nights are quieter out here. Warmer once you’re inside. I want a taste. Close the door.”
HERO: “Kira…”
KIRA (determined): “Don’t get nervous now. I’ve been thinking about this since the tavern. Come here. I’m done waiting.”
Scene: Kira intimacy scene 3.
Action: She settles on the edge of the bed and looks back toward the doorway, eyes bright. Lantern light glows in the room.
Unlock: Memory saved — Kira Intimate.
No further unlock. Each scene ends independently.
