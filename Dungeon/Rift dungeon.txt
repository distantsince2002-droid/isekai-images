Abyss Rift
Rank A required. Six floors. No NPCs except the Hero and any companions present. Each encounter offers four choices. Only one is correct. Wrong choices fail the floor and force a restart of that floor. The Hero does not answer every line. Some floors may only offer direction.
Floor 1
Scene: Rift dungeon exterior.
Action: The rune arch stands before the swirling rift. The Hero steps through. No fight in this picture.
HERO: “Rank A. The arch is open. The rift is already pulling.”
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: Floating stones and purple light fill the first hall. A prism crawler is already on the bridge.
Scene: Rift dungeon prism crawler.
Action: The crawler lunges.
Choices:
1. Stand and block the front legs. (Fail — the crystal edges cut through.)
2. Slip left and cut the joint behind the leading leg. (Correct — the crawler drops.)
3. Throw a loose stone at its glowing eye. (Fail — the stone bounces and the crawler charges.)
4. Run back through the arch. (Fail — the rift pulls harder than the retreat.)
HERO: “Joint is open. It is down. Stay off the edge.”
Floor 2
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: The path splits around a floating stone. A fold beast is already low on the right.
HERO: “Left path looks solid. Right has the beast. The stones are moving.”
Choices:
1. Take the left path. (Correct — the beast cannot follow the narrow stone.)
2. Take the right path. (Fail — the beast closes the gap.)
3. Jump to a floating stone. (Fail — the stone drifts and the beast follows.)
4. Wait for the beast to pass. (Fail — it does not pass.)
HERO: “Left. The stone holds.”
Scene: Rift dungeon fold beast.
Action: The beast turns and charges the left path anyway.
Choices:
1. Meet the charge head-on. (Fail — the weight breaks the stance.)
2. Sidestep and cut the shoulder where the plates are cracked. (Correct — the beast drops.)
3. Climb a floating stone. (Fail — the stone tips and the beast pulls you down.)
4. Throw a crystal at its eyes. (Fail — it does not flinch.)
HERO: “Shoulder is open. It is down.”
Floor 3
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: A stalker is already moving between the floating islands ahead.
Scene: Rift dungeon stalker.
Action: The stalker lunges.
Choices:
1. Raise both arms and try to catch the claws. (Fail — the claws punch through.)
2. Drop and roll, cutting the arm joint as it passes. (Correct — the stalker crashes.)
3. Hide behind a floating pillar. (Fail — it smashes the pillar.)
4. Jump to another island. (Fail — it follows and the gap closes.)
HERO: “Arm is cut. It is not moving again.”
Floor 4
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: A void leech is already circling a floating platform.
Scene: Rift dungeon void leech.
Action: The leech dives.
Choices:
1. Stand on the platform and wait. (Fail — the mouth closes on the stone.)
2. Step off the platform and cut the fin as it passes. (Correct — the leech drops.)
3. Throw a stone into the rift. (Fail — the rift only pulls harder.)
4. Climb the nearest pillar. (Fail — the leech reaches farther.)
HERO: “Fin is cut. It is down.”
Floor 5
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: A gargoyle of rift-stone is already perched on a broken arch.
Scene: Rift dungeon gargoyle.
Action: The gargoyle dives.
Choices:
1. Block the dive with a shield. (Fail — the stone breaks the rim.)
2. Roll under and cut the wing joint. (Correct — the gargoyle crashes.)
3. Hide under the arch. (Fail — it smashes the arch.)
4. Run across the bridge. (Fail — it is faster in the air.)
HERO: “Wing is open. It is down.”
Floor 6
Scene: Rift dungeon interior.
Action: The final platform. The abyss lord rises, tentacles already moving.
Scene: Rift dungeon Boss abyss lord.
Action: The lord lunges.
Choices:
1. Cut the glowing runes on the tentacles one by one. (Correct — the body collapses when the last rune is severed.)
2. Attack only the central mass. (Fail — the tentacles close while the centre stays solid.)
3. Jump to a floating stone and wait. (Fail — the tentacles pull the stone down.)
4. Run for the far arch. (Fail — the tentacles close the path.)
HERO: “The runes are cut. The tentacles are falling. The rift is quiet.”
No unlock. The entrance is open again.
